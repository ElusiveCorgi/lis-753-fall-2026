About These Programs

All programs are open to everyone. Registration is recommended because seating is limited and cannot be guaranteed without a reservation. Culinary programs require in-person attendance.

Accessibility

The Library makes every effort to ensure our programs can be enjoyed by all. If you have accessibility concerns or need to request a specific accommodation, please contact the Library.

Food Allergies and Dietary Restrictions

Food is served during culinary programs. Please list any food allergies or dietary restrictions in the comment section of the registration form. We may not be able to accommodate restrictions that are not disclosed at the time of registration.
