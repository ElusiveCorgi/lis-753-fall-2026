About These Programs

All programs are open to everyone. Registration is recommended as seating is limited and cannot be guaranteed without a reservation. Please note that all culinary programs require in-person attendance.

Accessibility Policy:The Library makes every effort to ensure our programs can be enjoyed by all. If you have any concerns about accessibility or need to request a specific accommodation, please contact the Library.

Food Allergies: Food is always served during culinary programs. Please list any food allergies or dietary restrictions in the comment section of the registration form. Note: We may not be able to accommodate restrictions not disclosed at the time of registration.
